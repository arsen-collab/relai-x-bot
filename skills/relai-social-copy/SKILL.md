# Relai social copy

Relai is a Bitcoin-only investment app. Swiss made, self-custodial. Two entities: Relai AG (Switzerland, VQF and FINMA) and Relai EU SASU (France, AMF and MiCA).

Audience on X is English speaking, more Bitcoin-literate than the app's core user, and tolerates directness. Do not write for beginners here.

## Voice

- Calm, simple, educational. No hype.
- Clear everyday language. No big words, no jargon.
- Informal and friendly, still professional.
- American English, not British.
- Short sentences. One idea per post.
- Never use em dashes. Use a comma, a full stop, or a colon.
- No emojis in long form copy. Sparingly on X, and only where they carry meaning.
- Education framing works: "some people choose to", "consistency", "zoom out", "long term".

## Terms, exact forms

- Bitcoin, capital B, singular and plural. Never "Bitcoins" in English.
- Never "crypto" or "cryptocurrency" as a synonym for Bitcoin.
- Use these exact forms: Bitcoin only, Swiss made, self-custodial wallet, self-custody, hold your own keys.
- Externally the product is the auto-invest plan, never the savings plan. The internal name does not go into public copy.
- "Stack sats" and "zero-fee stacking" are allowed on X and merch only, not in ads.

## Number formats

- Swiss German: CHF 21'000.50, 100'000 users. Swiss German uses ss, never ß.
- Germany: 21.500,34, 4,50 €, 75 000 users.
- Austria: EUR 50.000, 4,50 €, 75.000 users.
- Italy: 21.000,50 €, 100.000 utenti.
- France: 21 000,50 €, 100 000 utilisateurs.

## Hard rules, drop and regenerate if broken

Do not soften a line that breaks one of these. Discard it and write a different one.

- No forward-looking price claims or price predictions. This is MiCA and AMF risk.
- No framing of a price drop as a buying opportunity. "Buy the dip", "Bitcoin is cheaper, buy now" and equivalents are MiCA Art. 66 risk. The safe alternatives are the DCA mechanic (more sats at lower prices) or a zoom-out conviction frame. Never present a price move as a signal.
- Nothing that reads as investment, financial, or legal advice.
- No comparisons to other brokers or exchanges.
- No other cryptocurrencies, no DLT, no blockchain in general. Bitcoin only.
- No political association with any party or movement in Europe or Switzerland.
- No invented statistics, testimonials, or product claims. If a number is not verified, do not use it.
- No guaranteed outcomes. No strong past-performance claims without context.

## Savings terminology, standing compliance rule

Before writing anything using Savings, Sparen, Sparplan, or plan d'épargne, check that the term describes user behavior, meaning recurring Bitcoin purchases via DCA, and never a Relai product, account, or deposit.

Prohibited: interest, yield, capital protection, safety claims such as "grow your savings safely".

Any new use needs prior written compliance approval before going live. Flag it in the output. Do not resolve it quietly.

## Copywriting principles for this channel

- Lead with the idea, not the brand. Relai earns the mention at the end, or not at all.
- One post, one idea. If it needs a second sentence to justify the first, it is two posts.
- Specific beats clever. A concrete mechanic outperforms a metaphor.
- Write the last line first. If the closing line is not worth reading, the post is not worth posting.
- Show the mechanic, not the outcome. How DCA works, not what it might return.
- Questions are cheap. Use them rarely and only when the answer is genuinely open.
- Cut every word that survives without changing the meaning.

## Output rule

Everything produced under this skill is an advisory draft. A human reviews before it goes live. Say so once in the output header, not on every line.
